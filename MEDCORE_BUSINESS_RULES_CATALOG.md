| `FIN-007` | Commission Settlement Batch & Payout | `A / D` | `class-api-financial-settlement.php:45-430` | Finance admin settles accrued doctor commissions | `beneficiary_id`, `posting_ids[]` or `amount`, `shift_id`, `payment_method` | Locks unpaid `COMMISSION_ACCRUAL` postings, creates row in `mc_commission_settlements`, writes `SETTLE-BEN-*` (`DEBIT` `PAYABLE_COMMISSION`) and `SETTLE-TR-*` (`CREDIT` `TREASURY_CASH`) | Transitions accrued postings to settled, reduces doctor payable balance, reduces treasury cash | Rejects if payout exceeds unpaid accrued balance or no open shift | Currently writes `DEBIT` on `SETTLE-TR-*` (`FIN-BUG-01`) | Fix `SETTLE-TR-*` to `CREDIT` in Local MedCore Service. |
